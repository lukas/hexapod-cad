# Angle-tolerant RP-C10 sensor-foot BOM

| Quantity | Item | Notes |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | `fsr_foot_housing.stl` | PETG or PA; rigid housing and guide bores |
| 1 | `fsr_guided_carriage.stl` | PETG or PA; rigid spherical core, guide pins, and stop flange |
| 1 | `fsr_tpu_tread.stl` | TPU 95A, 1.0 mm spherical grip skin with captive snap lip |
| 1 | `fsr_tpu_sensing_spring.stl` | TPU 95A, three-spoke compliance/return flexure |

| 1 | 2.5 mm miniature zip tie | Retains the split sleeve on the Ø8 tube |
| ~0.5 m | 28–30 AWG flexible wire pair | Leave a service loop at the knee |
| 1 | 10 kΩ resistor | Initial voltage-divider value only; tune from measured resistance |
| small | 0.05–0.20 mm smooth PET/Kapton shim | Sets the sensor approach after printing |
| small | Thin double-sided tape | Inactive sensor rim/terminal retention only |

No nonstandard screw length is required. One four-sensor package supports a
three-foot tripod diagnostic plus one spare; a complete robot needs six.
